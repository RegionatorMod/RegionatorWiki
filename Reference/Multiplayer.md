# Multiplayer

Tested on a dedicated server with one guest. Two players at once, and hosting from your own game,
are not tested yet. Reports are welcome.

## What to expect

- Selections, the HUD and keys are your own; several players can use the tool at once.
- Costs and refunds go through the confirming player's [targeted
  containers](Container-Targeting) and inventory. If a player has the game's No Build
  Cost on, their own operations are free. Each player's auto pillar setting is their own.
- The host's limits apply to everyone. See [Limits and Known Issues](Limits-And-Known-Issues).
- A guest can have up to three operations running at once. Past that, the HUD asks you to wait
  for one to finish.
- If a large selection does not reach the host within 30 seconds, the HUD says so; confirm again.
- Before the blueprint milestone, the blueprint menu is unlocked while any player present has
  [Save Blueprint](Save-Blueprint) enabled.
