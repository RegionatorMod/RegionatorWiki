# Blueprints and Cost

[Move](Move), [Copy](Copy) and [Save Blueprint](Save-Blueprint) all work through a normal game
blueprint, the same kind the Blueprint Designer makes. This page covers what it carries and what
it costs.

## Cost

A blueprint costs its buildings' build cost plus the machines' power shards and Somersloops, so a
copy keeps its overclock. Belt items are never included. A Move is free; a Copy and a placement
from the blueprint menu pay the full cost each time. [Auto pillars](Auto-Pillars) add the cost of
their extra pieces. Under the game's No Build Cost, placing a blueprint costs nothing.

## Include items in Blueprint

**Mods > Regionator > General > Include items in Blueprint** (off by default) also saves what
machines and containers hold, and adds those items to the cost. A Move always carries contents,
whatever this setting.

## Auto-Connect

With the game's Auto-Connect on, a blueprint hologram whose open belt, pipe, hypertube or track
end comes within 1.5 m of a matching free connection shifts so the two meet exactly and join. This
works for every blueprint, but not while the hologram is locked.

## SnapOn

Splitters and mergers snapped on with SnapOn keep feeding their machines when copied. Blueprints
saved with an older version of Regionator, or with the Blueprint Designer, may not: save them again
with Save Blueprint.

## Large blueprints

Blueprints of 100 or more buildings are placed without the game's build effect, so they go up
faster.
