# Snap to Origin

Puts a [Move](Move) or [Copy](Copy) hologram exactly on its original buildings, locked, so you can
nudge it a known distance.

## How to use it

1. With a Move or Copy hologram up, press **Regionator: snap to origin** (`Left Ctrl + H`, or
   `L3 + R3` on a controller).
2. The hologram locks onto the originals.
3. Nudge it, then place it. Nudges follow the buildings' own axes, even off the world grid.

## Notes

- Pressed again after nudging, the key puts the hologram back on the originals, undoing the
  nudges.
- After you place a snapped Copy, the next one starts where the last was placed, so nudge and
  place makes evenly spaced rows. Unlock with the game's lock key to place freely.
- A Copy is red while it sits on its originals; nudge it off first. A Move can be placed on them.
- The game limits how far a locked hologram can be nudged; Infinite Nudge lifts that limit.

## Start at origin

**Start at origin**, under Move and under Copy in the Mods menu, starts every placement snapped on
the originals without the key press.
