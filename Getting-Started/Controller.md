# Controller and Steam Deck

The tool works like the build gun on a pad: primary fire places and confirms, secondary fire
steps back, and the build-mode button switches the tool mode. Equip it from the build menu or the
hotbar radial.

## Chords

Every Regionator command is a chord: hold `L3` and press:

- `LB` to equip the tool or put it away.
- `X` to open the [type filter](Type-Filtering).
- `RB` to [target the container](Container-Targeting) you are aiming at.
- `A` to [add another region](Regions).
- `Y` to switch between a box and a [sphere](Regions).
- `R3` for [Snap to Origin](Snap-To-Origin).
- `LT` to mark [Auto Pillars](Auto-Pillars).
- `B` to switch the nudge target while the region is locked (the D-pad nudges then).

Apart from equip, chords only apply while the tool, a blueprint hologram or the dismantle tool is
in hand, so sprinting and jumping are unaffected. Rebind them on the controller page of the
options menu.

## Popups

The Regionator popups work like the game's own: the left stick moves between fields, `A` chooses
or opens the on-screen keyboard, and `B` cancels. A hint row at the bottom lists the buttons.
